# Enchantments+

**Enchantments+** expands Minecraft's enchanting system with a huge collection of unique custom enchantments designed to add new mechanics, playstyles, and utility to your gear.

From powerful combat effects and defensive abilities to movement upgrades, farming tools, mining utilities, and quality-of-life improvements, Enchantments+ gives you more ways to customize your equipment and build the playstyle you want.

## ✨ Features

* **50+ custom enchantments** with unique effects
* New combat mechanics such as **Back Stab, Execute, Fatal Tempo** and **Necrotic Touch**
* Powerful defensive enchantments including **Fortress, Resilience** and **Heavy Plating**
* Movement and mobility upgrades such as **Kangaroo, Long Legs** and **Momentum Shift**
* Ranged combat improvements including **Precision, Snipe, Overload** and **Wind Blessing**
* Mining and gathering utilities such as **Vein Miner, Lumberjack** and **Compacting**
* Farming-focused enchantments including **Delicate, Replenish** and **Silent Harvest**
* Survival and exploration abilities such as **Ender Friend, Enlightenment** and **Growth**
* Loot and progression enhancements including **Divine Gift, Experienced** and **Scavenger**

## ⚔️ Combat

Take your weapons beyond vanilla enchantments.

**Back Stab** increases damage when attacking enemies from behind, while **First Strike** rewards the opening hit. **Triple Strike** boosts the first three hits against an enemy, and **Execute** and **Prosecute** scale your damage according to the target's health.

Other enchantments introduce entirely new combat mechanics:

* **Chain Lightning** — chance to unleash lightning that bounces between nearby targets.
* **Lightning Lord** — periodically strikes enemies with lightning.
* **Inferno** — every tenth hit stuns your enemy and deals heavy damage over time.
* **Poisonous Blade** — applies Poison II.
* **Necrotic Touch** — grants Life Steal against mobs.
* **Blood Thirst** — temporarily increases your damage after a kill.
* **Fatal Tempo** — temporarily increases Ferocity after hitting an enemy.
* **Combat Knowledge** — gives a tiny chance to instantly kill an enemy.
* **Starvation** — causes hit players to gain food exhaustion.

## 🛡️ Defense & Survival

Give your armor and shields abilities that vanilla Minecraft doesn't provide.

**Fortress** protects against attacks from all angles while your shield is active, while **Thorny** gives shields a mechanic similar to Thorns. **True Protection** reduces incoming true damage, and **Magic Protection** reduces damage from sources such as potions, dragon's breath, and sonic booms.

You can also improve survivability with **Growth**, **Endurance**, **Rejuvenate**, **Resilience**, and **Heavy Plating**.

## 🏹 Ranged Combat

Enhance your bows with specialized enchantments.

**Aim** makes arrows home in on nearby enemies in a small radius. **Precision** reduces bow inaccuracy, while **Snipe** increases damage based on how far the arrow travels. **Faster Arrows** increases projectile speed, and **Wind Blessing** combines increased projectile speed with arrows that ignore gravity.

**Overload** trades Draw Speed for additional Crit Damage and gives critical hits a chance to deal even more damage.

## ⛏️ Mining & Gathering

Make resource collection faster and more convenient.

* **Vein Miner** — breaks adjacent blocks of the same type when mining.
* **Lumberjack** — fells entire trees and other wood with a single block break.
* **Smelting Touch** — instantly smelts drops.
* **Telekinesis** — sends mob and block drops directly into your inventory.
* **Compacting** — automatically compacts dropped items into ingot or block forms.
* **Silent Harvest** — removes block-breaking sounds and particles.

## 🌾 Farming & Fishing

Add useful tools to your farming setup.

**Replenish** automatically replaces broken crop blocks, while **Delicate** prevents immature crops from being broken. **Chromatic** gives wool obtained from sheep random colors.

For fishing, **Flash** makes the fish reach the bobber faster. Combined with Lure, it can make fishing almost instant.

## 🌎 Exploration & Movement

Travel through the world in new ways.

**Basalt Walker** lets you walk across lava, while **Kangaroo** grants double jumps. **Long Legs** increases your Step Height, allowing you to walk up higher blocks without jumping.

**Ender Friend** prevents Endermen from targeting you when you look at them, and **Enlightenment** improves visibility by increasing fog distance and night vision while also reducing the Darkness effect.

## 💎 Utility & Progression

Enchantments+ also includes a variety of smaller upgrades that can make everyday gameplay more rewarding.

**Divine Gift** improves Luck, **Experienced** increases Wisdom and therefore experience gained from mobs and blocks, and **Scavenger** gives mobs a chance to drop emeralds or other special loot.

Other utility enchantments include **Gluttonous** for faster eating, **Insomnia** to prevent sleeping, **Sleepy** to prevent Phantoms from spawning, **Health Mending** to turn healing into tool repairs, and **Vampirism** to let wolves heal from damage they deal.
